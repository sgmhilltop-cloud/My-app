Energy accounting rules

KPLC consumption = Main KPLC incomer consumption − Solar meter consumption.

Standard unit: all stored values are normalized to kWh.

Rate: KSh 14.50 per kWh (editable by Admin).

A daily consumption figure requires a previous reading for the same meter. Counter resets/rollovers should be reviewed by Admin before approval.

Enter meter reading

Electricians and Data Clerk can save new readings but cannot edit a saved reading. Duplicate readings for the same meter and date are blocked.

Camera / image OCR

Take a meter photo or upload an existing image. OCR is a suggestion; verify the extracted number and date before saving.

Photo metadata may not contain the meter reading date. The app attempts to read a recognizable date printed in the image text; many meter photos do not show a date, so enter or verify the date manually.
